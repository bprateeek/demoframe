# P4 primitive acceptance

P4 implements only the primitive set selected by the approved capability-gap
memo: kinetic text, logo lockup, persistent product surface/state, hero metric,
chart path, and a sanitized local image/SVG object. Cursor, orbit/sonar,
floating-perspective, unrestricted callout-grid, and decorative mascot systems
remain outside the contract.

The acceptance story is
[`examples/primitives/demo.yml`](../../examples/primitives/demo.yml): a clean
Pulseboard hook, one degraded service surface, the same surface recovering, and
a 99.99% payoff with a manifest-backed brand lockup.
