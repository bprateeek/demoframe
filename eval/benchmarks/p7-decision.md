# P7 audio decision: not shipped

P7 was explicitly optional. It is not part of the three user-approved timed
animatics or the approved capability-gap memo, and none of the P0-P6 acceptance
stories requires sound to communicate its causal flow.

Adding local music/SFX, beat alignment, fades, deterministic PCM mixing, format
policy, licensing/provenance, and silent-profile compatibility would introduce
a second media timeline without improving the approved README, dashboard, or
product-tour outcomes. That conflicts with the governing design direction:
clarity and purpose over extra elements.

Decision: keep every current profile silent and leave P7 unimplemented. Audio
can be reconsidered only when an approved social-film benchmark demonstrates a
specific comprehension or pacing gain that visuals alone cannot provide. This
is a completed scope decision, not a technical blocker.
